# Directory listings for MLAIA

Research and draft profile text for third-party listings. **Yochai creates every account, accepts the terms and submits.** Nothing here has been submitted.

Researched on 2 November 2026. Costs and link attributes change, so check each one when you sign up. "Dofollow?" says what the directory itself states. Where that isn't documented, it says *unverified*. To check, open any live profile, right-click the website link, choose Inspect, and look for `rel="nofollow"`, `ugc` or `sponsored`.

Even a nofollow profile is worth having when it ranks for searches like "AI consultancy Israel" or "audio DSP consultants", or when AI assistants use it as a source. ChatGPT is already MLAIA's top referrer, and these assistants draw on Clutch, Crunchbase and similar directories.

### Specialist directories

Audio, acoustics and medical AI have no open, free vendor directory that fits MLAIA well:

- **Audio and acoustics.** The NCAC directory (https://ncac.com/directory) and similar lists are for US building and environmental acoustics firms, and you need to be a member. The COMSOL certified-consultant list needs COMSOL certification. Edge AI and Vision Alliance (https://www.edge-ai-vision.com) membership is paid and aimed at chip and tool vendors. AES (https://aes.org) has a sustaining-member listing that is also paid. **Recommendation:** skip these for now. Revisit Edge AI and Vision if an edge-audio client comes from there.
- **Medical AI.** The visible directories (OpenCurious, The Medical Futurist's top 100, KLAS) are curated lists of product companies, and there's no self-serve submission. Israeli MedTech bodies, such as the IATI member directory, need paid membership. **Recommendation:** skip for now. The Scholar and ORCID profiles and the eLife paper do more for credibility in this field.

It is led by Dr. Yochai Edlitz (Ph.D., Weizmann Institute of Science), who has more than 20 years of R&D experience and is a co-inventor on granted patents in acoustic signal processing and machine learning.

> We work in three fields:
>
> **Audio, acoustics and signal processing.** Classical DSP and machine learning engineered together: sound event detection, noise reduction and active noise cancellation, microphone arrays and beamforming, vibration analysis for predictive maintenance, and audio models that run on phones, DSPs and embedded controllers.
>
> **Medical AI and clinical data science.** Models for clinical records, medical images and biosignals that a clinical reviewer can evaluate: leakage checks, patient and site splits, external validation, calibration and subgroup analysis, plus de-identification of clinical data.
>
> **Forecasting and causal analysis.** Item-level demand forecasting, including slow and intermittent items, churn prediction, and causal analysis of which actions actually change an outcome, with the assumptions stated.
>
> We take a project from the research question to production with the client's team. We agree success metrics before development, set classical baselines before adding learned models, and measure every model before it's deployed.
