What an AI agent can do for you

What can an agent actually do for us?

Three things, mostly:

  • Answer from your approved documents, with sources.
  • Act in your CRM and tools, with approval for anything that matters.
  • Speak Hebrew or English, on the web or the phone.

And how do you build it?

LangGraph for the logic, Amazon Bedrock for the models, n8n for the workflows. We test the answers on real examples before your customers see them.
