The step up is in the evidence. Prediction alone does not establish the effect of an action. Causal conclusions depend on study design, data and assumptions; counterfactual conclusions can require stronger assumptions still. We make those assumptions and limitations explicit.

How an engagement works

Questions buyers ask

Good questions. Straight answers.

How accurate will the forecast be?

We can’t promise a number before we see your data. We measure it with backtests on your own history, next to your current method, so you know the error before you rely on it.

Our demand is sparse and intermittent. Can you forecast it?

Yes. Slow, sporadic demand is what we built NextDemand for. Methods such as Croston’s model treat when demand happens separately from how much, which standard ERP min/max rules don’t. Read why min/max fails.

Prediction vs causal: what’s the difference for us?

Prediction tells you which customers are likely to churn or which items will run out. Causal analysis tells you whether an action, such as a retention offer or an earlier reorder, changes that outcome. A high risk score alone doesn’t show an offer will work.

Will it work with our ERP or CRM data?

Usually, yes. We start from exports of your CRM, ERP or operational history. Data quality, coverage and the planning horizon decide what’s realistic, and the data audit tells you early.

What do you need to start?

The decision you want to support, a description of the data you have, and how you forecast today. For the first call, a schema or column list is enough. Please don’t send confidential data.

Is this the same as NextDemand?

No. NextDemand is MLAIA’s own product for spare-parts demand and inventory planning. This practice builds bespoke forecasting, churn and causal models for your business.
