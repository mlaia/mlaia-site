01 What we do

From the map to production.

Start with one service or go the whole way. Each one ends with something your team can keep working with.

01

We map where AI pays off

We go through the organisation’s processes, teams and data and rank the use cases by expected value, complexity and risk. You get a focused list of what to do first and what to leave for later.

02

We choose the tools

Microsoft Copilot, ChatGPT Enterprise, Claude, or agents built in-house. We work out what fits each team, by the kind of work, the systems already in place, security requirements and cost.

03

An AI usage and data policy

We write the usage policy with you: which tools are approved, what information may and may not go into them, and who approves new uses. It is aligned with Amendment 13 of Israel’s Privacy Protection Law and with the company’s information-security rules.

04

We build people’s AI skills

Hands-on training by role: sales, customer service, finance, HR, engineering and management. Each team practises on tasks from its own work, in the tools the organisation has approved.

05

We build AI skills and workflows for teams

We build reusable Claude or Copilot skills and agents, tied to the team’s own tools and data. A task that comes up again and again is done the same way every time, not by whichever prompt someone remembers.

06

Pilots with success measures, then production

Before anything is rolled out, we run a defined pilot on a specific team and process, with success measures agreed up front. What works goes into production with permissions, monitoring and clear ownership. What doesn’t is stopped.

07

Cost savings

The aim is to find the work and spend that AI can take over, and cut it. We map repetitive manual tasks and outside services that could be replaced, and measure the before and after in the pilot, so the decision rests on your own data.

04 How an engagement runs

From the map to working use.

Five steps, each with a clear output. You can stop after any of them.

01

Map

Conversations with management and team leads, and a review of processes and data. Output: a ranked list of use cases.

02

Choose

Matching tools to teams, including a security, permissions and licensing-cost review. Output: a reasoned recommendation per team.

03

Policy and training

A usage and data policy approved on your side, and training by role. Output: a policy document, and teams starting to work by it.

04

Pilot

A defined team and process, success measures set in advance and a before-and-after measurement. Output: a decision to continue, change or stop.

05

Production

What worked is scaled: integration, permissions, monitoring and internal ownership. Output: a workflow your team owns.

05 Questions buyers ask

Straight answers.

Our people already use ChatGPT or Copilot. What else is needed?

Usually three things: a clear policy, training that fits each role, and workflows built around the tool instead of ad-hoc use. We start from what you already have.

Which tool do you recommend?

It depends on the team. The recommendation follows the kind of work, the systems already in use, security requirements and cost, and sometimes the answer is a mix of tools.

Will the policy you write meet Amendment 13 of the Privacy Protection Law?

We write the policy to align with the requirements of Amendment 13 and with your information-security rules. We are not a law firm, so the final wording should be approved by your legal counsel and whoever owns information security in the organisation.

Does our data leave the organisation?

Only as far as the policy allows. For each tool we check where data is processed and stored and which organisation-level settings it offers, and we build inside the environments you have approved.

What do you need from us to start?

A contact in management, a list of the tools already in use, and time for short conversations with a few team leads. A first intro call is enough to decide whether and how to start.
