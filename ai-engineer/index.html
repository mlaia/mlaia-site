05 Common questions

Straight answers.

Do you replace hiring?

No. You can start now and keep hiring in parallel. When your hire arrives we hand over the work, code and documentation, and you can keep us on as a supporting team.

Does our data leave the organisation?

It does not have to. The solutions can be built and run in your own cloud or on-prem, using only tools and models your organisation approved.

Which organisations is this for?

Any organisation with processes and data, including ones that are not tech companies: insurance and finance, health and pharma, manufacturing, retail and import, transport and the public sector.

Is there a minimum commitment?

We start with one month of mapping and a pilot. At the end of it we decide together whether to continue, and at what scope.

Who is the engineer?

An AI engineer from the MLAIA team. Behind them are AI engineers from Israel's leading universities, and AI leads with more than 20 years in development and management roles.
