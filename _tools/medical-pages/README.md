# Medical AI topic pages: source

Source for the three topic pages under `/medical-ai/`:
`clinical-model-validation`, `medical-imaging-ai` and `clinical-data-deidentification`.

The pages are generated by the shared renderer in `_tools/audio-pages/render_audio.py`.
Each JSON sets `"practice": "medical-ai"`. Medical pages also set:

- `cta_href` and `nav_cta`: the calls to action go to the feasibility-call form at `/medical-ai/#contact`.
- `contact`: a call-to-action band replaces the per-page form.
- `proofline`: the hero credentials line (cites the eLife 2022 paper).
- a `sources` section with `reads`: every external standard or paper cited on the page.

Don't hand-edit the page HTML. Edit the JSON here and rebuild from the repository root:

```bash
python3 _tools/audio-pages/render_audio.py . _tools/medical-pages/content/*.json
```

The content rules in `_tools/audio-pages/README.md` apply here too, plus:

- Cite only links that were checked to resolve to the named paper or standard.
- MLAIA produces validation evidence. It is not a regulatory, legal or privacy-compliance consultant; keep that explicit.
- Don't contradict the `#deidentification` section of `medical-ai/index.html`: de-identification is not a guarantee of anonymity.
- Name clients only as the medical practice page already does.

A new topic page also needs a card in the `#topics` section of `medical-ai/index.html`, a share card in `_tools/share-images/make_cards.py`, and entries in `sitemap.xml` and `llms.txt`.
